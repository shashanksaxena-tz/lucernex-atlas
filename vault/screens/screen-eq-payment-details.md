**The largest subtraction in the module.** `Payment Info` drops five of [[Contract]]'s twelve screens:
`Alternate Rent`, `Invoices`, `Recoveries`, `Percentage Rent`, `Sales`.

Every one is retail: [[ExpenseRecovery|recoveries]] are landlord expense pass-through,
[[PercentageRent|percentage rent]] and [[Sales|sales]] are turnover rent.

**And the rename is the tell.** `Recurring Expenses` → `Recurring Payments` is not a dropped screen but
a reframed one — the same [[ExpenseSetup]] machinery, described as an outflow rather than a recovery.
