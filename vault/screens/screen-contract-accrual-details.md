[[equipment-contract|Equipment Contract]]**, which is the clearest signal in the navigation diff that
accruals belong to the retail-property layer rather than to generic lease accounting.

The four: `Accrual Details`, `Expense Accruals`, `Transactions`, `Percentage Rent Accruals`.
