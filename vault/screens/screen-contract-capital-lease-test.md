The **ASC 840** test, stored on [[Contract]] itself as `Test1Result`…`Test5aResult`/`Test5bResult` —
distinct from the newer [[ContractFinancialTest]] record behind `ASC 842 Test`.

**Two generations of the same idea coexist**, and the renderer split reflects the difference:
this is a **detail form** because ASC 840 produces one result per contract, while `ASC 842 Test` is a
**list** because it produces many rows ([[finding-two-files-serve-56-percent]]).

**It is dropped from [[equipment-contract|Equipment Contract]] while `ASC 842 Test` is kept** — and
that is the one dropped item the generic-versus-retail reading does not explain:
[[q-bbw-02-capital-lease-test]].

Careful with the polarity here as everywhere: [[finding-classification-polarity-inverted]].
