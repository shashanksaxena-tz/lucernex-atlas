**`co_tenancy` · 27 fields · [[module-contracts]]**

The co-tenancy clause — rent reduction if an anchor tenant vacates. **A mall clause**, and therefore
one of the cleanest markers of what is retail-property-specific rather than generic lease accounting.

That is why it is **dropped from [[equipment-contract|Equipment Contract]]**, and why its presence in
a rebuild's scope is a question about what kind of product ASG Edge+ is.

[[tenant-american-freight|American Freight]] retains two older co-tenancy layout variants
(`ASG Co Tenancy`, `ASG Contract Co Tenancy`) that [[tenant-bbw|BBW]] has replaced with
`ASG Contract Cotenants` and `ASG Cotenancy Language` — one of the few genuine
[[finding-publish-then-fork|drift]] cases that is not the AI pipeline.

`Cotenancy Update` is one of BBW's 13 [[WorkFlowTemplate|workflow templates]] (id 2478, 2 steps).
