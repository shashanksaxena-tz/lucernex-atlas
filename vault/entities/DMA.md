**`d_m_a` · 7 fields · [[module-facilities-locations]]**

Designated Market Area — the reference record behind site-selection demographics. Referenced by 10
objects, which is high for a seven-field lookup.

Part of the nine-object demographics family, which has **no ASG Edge+ counterpart** and is the
facilities module's clearest out-of-scope candidate.

Not to be confused with `Market Area Code` (`TableType 2049`), a 213-value code table that is a
`MARKET` routing level and **not** a [[Region]] level.
