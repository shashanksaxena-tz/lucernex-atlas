**`expense_accrual_schedule` · 31 fields · [[module-accounting]]**

`ExpenseAccrualSetup` in the same clause→schedule shape as [[ExpenseSetup]] → [[ExpenseSchedule]].

Part of the whole `Accrual Info` group that [[equipment-contract|Equipment Contract]] drops —
see [[AccrualTransaction]].
