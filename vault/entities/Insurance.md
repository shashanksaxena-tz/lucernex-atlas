**`insurance` · 27 fields · [[module-contracts]]**

Required insurance coverage terms and certificate dates on a lease. Survives into
[[equipment-contract|Equipment Contract]] untouched — it is generic to any lease, not retail-specific.

Its screen is one of the ten [[finding-routes-are-not-addressable|that bounce to the record shell]]
rather than resolving to a URL of their own.

`VendorInsurance` is a separate object covering the counterparty side.
