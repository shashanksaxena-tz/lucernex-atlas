**`sales_exclusion_cap` · 23 fields · variable-rent**

The cap limiting how much of reported [[Sales]] may be excluded from the [[PercentageRent]]
calculation — online sales, employee sales, returns.

Configured through `Exclusion Cap Code` (`TableType 2183`).

A small object that carries real money risk: an exclusion the rebuild models loosely changes turnover
rent directly.
