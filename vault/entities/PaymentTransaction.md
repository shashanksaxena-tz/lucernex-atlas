**`payment_transaction` · 118 fields · [[module-accounting]]**

The posted money: a rent or expense payment with split GL coding. The **transaction** layer of
[[setup-schedule-transaction]], discriminated by `PostingDate` + GL account + counterparty.

- `VendorID` is typed **`Employer ID`** — see [[Employer]]. "Vendor" is a relabelled Employer.
- `AppliedToPayTranID` is a self-reference.
- The GL export contract is `AccountNumber1..8`.
- **Five independent date axes must coexist**: posting, effective, due, coverage, settlement
  ([[rule-CON-R-103]]).

**A schema gap worth knowing before designing the payment model:** no foreign key and no link table
connects [[PaymentReceipt]] to `PaymentTransaction` — only allocated and unallocated totals
([[rule-CON-R-118]]). Cash application is not modelled.

It has a staging twin, `PaymentTransactionFullImport` (118 fields) — one of only two such twins in the
223 objects.

Generated by a **button**, not a batch job — see [[action-buttons]] and
[[finding-engine-is-button-driven]].
