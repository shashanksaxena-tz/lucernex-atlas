**`TAX-R-002`** · [[module-property-tax]] · **Observed**

[[PropertyTaxBill]] is **always** raised against a specific [[PropertyTaxAssessment]], never against
the [[PropertyTaxSummary]] directly. The dated assessment is the anchor for everything downstream.

See [[rules-property-tax]] for the full register.
