**`FAC-R-012`** · [[module-facilities-locations]] · **Observed**

Neither forces traversal through the other. A [[Contract]] can name a [[Facility]], a [[Location]], or
both — and a rebuild that derives one from the other will disagree with the data.

See [[rules-facilities-locations]] for the full register.
