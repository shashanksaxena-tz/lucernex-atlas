**`PPL-R-013`** · [[module-people-parties]] · **Derived**

[[Member]]'s **eight approval-amount band columns** are read by **no** observed workflow field.

Amount-banded approval routing is a documented ASG Edge+ requirement (`D10`) — and the incumbent has
the columns and does not use them. That is useful: it is a build, not a migration.
