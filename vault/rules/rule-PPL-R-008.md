**`PPL-R-008`** · [[module-people-parties]] · **Observed**

`MemberAudit` is **login, session and impersonation** audit — distinct from `AuditColumn`'s
field-change audit. Two of the three [[audit-trail|audit mechanisms]] in the product, measuring
different things.

See [[rules-people-parties]] for the full register.
