**`ACC-R-050`** · [[module-accounting]] · **Observed**

> **"Once approved, it cannot be un-approved."**

The terminus of a three-step approval workflow ([[rule-ACC-R-060]]). A wrong approval is corrected by
generating a new schedule, not by undoing the old one.

See [[finding-schedules-are-approved-not-published]].
