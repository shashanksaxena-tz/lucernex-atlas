**`PLT-R-007`** · [[module-platform-tenancy]] · **Observed**

Level ∈ `DEFAULT`, `NO_ACCESS`, `VIEW`, `EDIT`, `DELETE`, and a grant targets **exactly one** of Page
Access, Actions, Field Security or Budget Columns.

**`DEFAULT` means *inherit*, not *allowed*** — the trap that destroyed a confident hypothesis. See
[[security-ladder]].

See [[rules-platform-tenancy]] for the full register.
