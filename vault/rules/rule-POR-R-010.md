**`POR-R-010`** · [[module-portfolio-transactions]] · **Derived**

[[Project]] and [[Contract]] are **independent [[subtype-root|subtype roots]] with independent
financial engines**. A capital project and a lease on the same building do not share a spine node.

See [[rules-portfolio-transactions]] for the full register.
