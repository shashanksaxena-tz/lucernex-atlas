**`DOC-R-011`** · [[module-documents-folders]] · **Observed**

Received-email attachments are **promoted into ordinary [[Document]]/[[Folder]] records**. There is
**no equivalent for sent mail** — `EMailSentLog` is a one-field stub.

So the product remembers what came in and not what went out.

See [[rules-documents-folders]] for the full register.
