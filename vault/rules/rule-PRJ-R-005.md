**`PRJ-R-005`** · [[module-projects-capital]] · **Derived**

[[Program|Portfolio]]'s default `HolidaySchedule` and its weekend policy feed **task duration
arithmetic**, overridable per task.

**This is the correction that moves the holiday calendar out of accounting.**
[[screen-manage-holiday-calendar|Manage Holiday Calendar]] sits among the five financial
reference-data tools on the admin dashboard and belongs with [[TaskGroup|scheduling]] — its own help
text says holiday days determine task completion dates.

The calendar is **empty** in [[tenant-bbw|BBW]], as four of those five tables are —
[[feature-reference-data]].

`Holiday Calendar` is also one of the ten [[soft-reference|soft reference types]], appearing on just 3
columns.

See [[rules-projects-capital]] for the full register.
