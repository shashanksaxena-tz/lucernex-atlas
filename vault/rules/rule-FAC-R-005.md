**`FAC-R-005`** · [[module-facilities-locations]] · **Observed**

[[Parcel]]'s containment parent is [[Location]] (required); its [[Facility]] link is **optional** — as
are its [[Contract]], [[Complex]], [[Prototype]] and [[Organization]] links. **Five simultaneous
optional parents.**
