**`ACC-R-009`** · [[module-accounting]] · **Observed**

Test 4 fails if `InitialLiabilityBalance > FairValueOfAsset × PortionControlled × FairValueThreshold`
(usually 90%).

**Which liability balance is meant is unresolved** — and since a *fail* produces a Finance lease
([[rule-ACC-R-011]]), getting it wrong changes the balance-sheet treatment.

On [[ContractFinancialTest]].

See [[rules-accounting]] for the full register.
