**`POR-R-011`** · [[module-portfolio-transactions]] · **Observed**

**No column** traces a [[Facility]] or [[Location]] back to its originating [[PotentialProject]] — and
`PotentialProject` has **zero inbound edges** in the 972-edge graph.

**Observed by absence.** The pre-lease pipeline is unreconstructible from the records it produces.

See [[rules-portfolio-transactions]] for the full register.
