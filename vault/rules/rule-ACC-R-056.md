**`ACC-R-056`** · [[module-accounting]] · **Observed**

[[Program]] carries **three independent Per-Day / Per-Period amortisation switches** — one for the
asset, one for cash, one for expense.

A rebuild with a single amortisation setting cannot express a portfolio that amortises the asset
per-day and the expense per-period, which this product permits.

See [[rules-accounting]] for the full register.
