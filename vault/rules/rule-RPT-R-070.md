**`RPT-R-070`** · [[module-reporting]] · **Observed**

> **An ASG Edge+ requirement, not an Lx rule.** Priority: **Blocker**.

Restrict edit access to standard reports (e.g. the ASC 842 schedule) while still allowing users to
apply filters at run time.

Note `RPT-R-070`…`077` are all requirements rather than observations, and `RPT-R-034`/`035` are
**retired and must not be reused**.
