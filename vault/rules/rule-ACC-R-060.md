**`ACC-R-060`** · [[module-accounting]] · **Observed**

A generated schedule is a **candidate**. It passes a three-step `ASC 842 Schedule Review/Approval`
workflow — review, ASG approve, client approve — before it counts.

*(The workflow is Observed; its link to `IsApproved` is Derived.)*
