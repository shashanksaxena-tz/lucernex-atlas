The principal selector (what kind of thing names the people) and the scope selector (where to search for them) are two independent dimensions, not two rival vocabularies for one idea. A rebuild should model routing as one reusable principal selector crossed with an orthogonal scope selector, and let workflow, notification and task assignment all consume the same mechanism — Lx already reuses this exact shape on NotifyTemplateMember and LinkTaskByCodeMember.
