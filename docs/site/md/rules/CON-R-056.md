# CON-R-056 — 5. Percentage rent

*Contracts & Leases · Derived*

**Net sales are computed: NetSalesPeriodAmount = GrossSalesPeriodAmount − ExcludedSalesPeriodAmount.**

Net sales are computed: NetSalesPeriodAmount = GrossSalesPeriodAmount − ExcludedSalesPeriodAmount.
