# WF-R-022 — B. Kick-off and instantiation

*Workflow & Approvals · Observed*

**If set, the parent workflow's ad-hoc assignee becomes the ad-hoc assignee of the first step of the newly spawned workflow.**

If set, the parent workflow's ad-hoc assignee becomes the ad-hoc assignee of the first step of the newly spawned workflow.
