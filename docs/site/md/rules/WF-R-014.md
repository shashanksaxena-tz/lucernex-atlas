# WF-R-014 — B. Kick-off and instantiation

*Workflow & Approvals · Observed*

**A workflow may be kicked off any of four ways per the GraphQL enum. This corrects the vendor help text, which names only three and omits STATUS_CHANGE.**

A workflow may be kicked off any of four ways per the GraphQL enum. This corrects the vendor help text, which names only three and omits STATUS_CHANGE.

## The wording it rests on

> There are three ways a work flow can be kicked off: by completion of an action in another work flow, by completion of a schedule task, or by completion of a form.
