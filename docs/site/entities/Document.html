The document/file metadata record used across the platform — author, checkout status (Checked Out By Member, Checked Out Date) for document locking during edits. 22 Global fields under Documents.

Source: data-fields/document.md

What to know before rebuilding this

Tenant-scoped, one join deep Derived

This record carries no FirmID of its own. It hangs off ProjectEntity, and tenant isolation has to be enforced by joining to that row and filtering on its FirmID — or it is not enforced at all. 161 of the 223 record types are shaped this way.
