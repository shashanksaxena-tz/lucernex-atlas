What to know before rebuilding this

5 tenant custom columns Observed

This record carries 5 physical Firm_-prefixed columns — tenant custom fields are real columns, not rows in a value store, so adding one is a DDL change. That is direct evidence for database-per-tenant and against a shared schema.

Tenant-scoped, one join deep Derived

This record carries no FirmID of its own. It hangs off ProjectEntity, and tenant isolation has to be enforced by joining to that row and filtering on its FirmID — or it is not enforced at all. 161 of the 223 record types are shaped this way.

4 catalogued Firm-scope fields Observed

Of 21 catalogued fields on this record, 4 are Firm scope — defined by this tenant rather than shipped by the platform. Firm-scope definitions are RGAF rows carrying IsGlobal, FirmID and IsClientExtensionField.
