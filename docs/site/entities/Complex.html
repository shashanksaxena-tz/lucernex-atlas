A multi-building property complex/campus record sitting above Facility in the property hierarchy — complex-level classification and status fields plus a linked Person (likely site or leasing contact). 45 Global fields under its own Complex group.

Source: data-fields/complex.md

What to know before rebuilding this

Firm-global reference data Derived

Owned by the firm as a whole rather than by any one business record — configuration and reference data rather than transactional rows.

Rules that govern it · 2

RuleWhat it requiresConfidence
FAC-R-008Input: `ComplexID` on `Facility`, `Location`, `Parcel`, `Prototype` (all `Required = No` where exposed) and `Competitor`. Effect: Any of the four subtype roots — and a tracked competitor — may be grouped under one sharedObserved
FAC-R-009Effect: `Complex`'s 45-field schema contains no FK back into `Location`/`Facility`/`Parcel`/ `Prototype`/`Program`, and no `ProjectEntityID`. It is `firm_global`, not entity-scoped.Derived
