Bid Package Ancillary Tables

These 6 tables (21 fields, all Global) are the line-item and alternate/breakout detail underneath the two large standalone bid entities, BidPackage (53 fields) and BidPackageTemplate (31 fields). Each pairs a 'definition' table (Breakout, Alternate) with a matching 'Value' table holding the bidder-submitted number against it — the same header/line-item pattern seen throughout the catalog, just at a smaller scale for the competitive-bid workflow specifically.
