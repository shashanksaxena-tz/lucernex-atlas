PPL-R-009

People & Parties · A member's routable identity is three independent axes

Observed

- Trigger: Any workflow, task, or notification routing rule. - Input: `Member.CodeUserClassID`, `Member.CodeJobTitleID` (default) / `LinkMemberProjectEntity.CodeJobTitleIDList`/`AssignedCodeJobTitleIDList` (per-entity override), `Member.SupervisorID` (org chart). - Condition: — - Effect: A person can be selected by class, by title (global or entity-specific), or by reporting-line position, and these three do not have to agree with each other. - Confidence: Observed — `security-model.md`, cross-referencing `../workflow/routing-and-approvals.md` §2 without re-deriving it.
