PPL-R-011

People & Parties · Region/Market routing resolves through the entity, not through any `Member` column

Derived

- Trigger: `AssigneeType = REGION1 | REGION2 | MARKET`. - Input: `ProjectEntity.RegionID`/`RootRegionID`/`SubRegionID`/`CodeMarketAreaID`/ `CodeMarketTypeID` (in `../platform-tenancy/`), then `LinkRegionManager` to find the responsible member. - Condition: — - Effect: This is a two-hop resolution (entity → region/market → manager), not a direct `Member` attribute lookup. - Confidence: Derived — `security-model.md`.
