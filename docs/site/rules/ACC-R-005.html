ACC-R-005

Lease Accounting & Payments · Test 1: title transfer

Observed

Title reverts to the tenant = Fail = finance lease.

- Trigger: classification test run. - Inputs: `ContractFinancialTest.DoesTitleRevertToTenant` (`sTYPE_CHECKBOX`). - Condition: `DoesTitleRevertToTenant = true` ⇒ Fail; false ⇒ Pass. - Output: `Test1Result` (`sTYPE_PASS_FAIL`). - Confidence: Observed that this checkbox *is* Test 1; Derived for the polarity, from the global rule *"If you 'fail' at least one of the five tests, the lease will be classified as a Finance lease."*
