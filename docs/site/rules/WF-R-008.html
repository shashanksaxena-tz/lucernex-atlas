WF-R-008

Workflow & Approvals · A. Template definition and availability

Observed

True selects a Form step, rendering a page layout and collecting a decision; false selects a Task step, working a schedule item with no form surface at all.

Administrator configures a step
`WFTS.IsFormStep`
—
The step is a Form step (true) or a Task step (false)
Observed — *"This field has one of two values: Form Step or Task Step."*
