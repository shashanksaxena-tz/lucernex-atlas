ACC-R-017

Lease Accounting & Payments · Accounting date window

Observed

Begin date is the later of the firm's adoption date and possession; end date includes likely options.

- Trigger: classification test creation. - Inputs: firm's ASC 842 adoption date; `Contract.PossessionBeginDate`; likely options. - Computation: `Topic842BeginDate = max(adoption date, PossessionBeginDate)`; `Topic842EndDate` = end of accounting including likely options. - Output: `Topic842BeginDate`, `Topic842EndDate`. - Confidence: Observed — *"This date is the date your organization is adopting ASC 842, or the Possession Begin Date, whichever is later."*
