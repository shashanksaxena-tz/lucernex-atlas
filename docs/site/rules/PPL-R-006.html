PPL-R-006

People & Parties · The per-entity contact roster and the per-entity vendor list are separate, differently-shaped joins

Observed

- Trigger: Listing "who is associated with this entity." - Input: `LinkProjectEntityContact` (12 fields, classified by `CodeContactTypeID`, carries a parallel `Landlord_*` pair) vs. `LinkProjectEntityVendor` (4 fields, unclassified — just `ProjectEntityID` + `VendorID`). - Condition: — - Effect: Contacts get a typed, richer roster entry; approved vendors get a bare membership list with no role classification at all. - Confidence: Observed, this module's field export. ## Audit and identity load
