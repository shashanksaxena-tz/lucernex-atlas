PLT-R-011

Platform & Tenancy · Exchange rates are point-in-time captures, not a live feed

Derived

- Trigger: A multi-currency Contract calculation. - Input: `ExchangeRate.ContractID`, `ConversionRate`, `EffectiveDate`. - Condition: — - Effect: The rate used for a calculation is whatever was captured effective as of a given date, not a re-derived live lookup — supports historical reporting without recomputation. - Confidence: Derived — field shape only, no live capture. ## Audit
