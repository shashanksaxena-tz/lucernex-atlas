WF-R-050

Workflow & Approvals · E. Approval decisions

Observed

Vendor definition of IsApprovalAction: "Specifies whether the current action is an approval or not. If it is not, then the step is either restarted or denied." The engine knows approve vs not-approve and nothing finer; denial and send-back are the same class, differing only in what RestartStep and MoveToStepNumber do next.
