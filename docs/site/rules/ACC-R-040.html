ACC-R-040

Lease Accounting & Payments · Gross asset balance

Observed

Asset balance plus accumulated amortisation.

- Computation: `GrossAssetBalance[n] = AssetAmount[n] + CumulativeAssetAmortExpense[n]`. - Confidence: Observed — *"This field's value is equal to the Asset Balance + the Accumulated Amortization Balance."*
