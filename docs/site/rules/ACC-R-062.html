ACC-R-062

Lease Accounting & Payments · Approver resolution

Observed

All three steps resolve their approver as a named Member, even though the platform also supports routing by job title or org-chart position.

Expected rule
Status
An IFRS 16 classification test
Does not exist. IFRS 16 has no lessee classification; Lucernex models none.
A short-term / low-value practical-expedient gate that skips schedule generation
Does not exist as a rule. `IsShortTerm` and `IsLowAssetValue` are stored on both `Contract` and `Asset` but nothing documents them driving any behaviour.
A straight-line suspension rule
Does not exist. `SuspendSL` is dead (ACC-R-055).
