ACC-R-008

Lease Accounting & Payments · Test 3 override: commencement near end of economic life

Observed

The 'lease is near end' tick box forces a Pass unconditionally.

- Trigger: after ACC-R-007. - Inputs: `IsLeaseNearEnd` (`sTYPE_CHECKBOX`). - Condition: `IsLeaseNearEnd = true` ⇒ `Test3Result := Pass`, unconditionally. - Output: `Test3Result`. - Confidence: Observed — *"If this check box is selected, Test 3's outcome will change to Pass regardless of whether the value the Test Term Length to Remaining Life field is greater than the Remaining Economic Life Threshold field."*
