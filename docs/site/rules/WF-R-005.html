WF-R-005

Workflow & Approvals · A. Template definition and availability

Derived

The workflow's own attachability is whatever its kick-off form's Form Type declares. WorkFlowTemplate carries no IsValidFor* columns of its own — the chain runs through PageLayoutID to the layout's Form Type.

A workflow template is bound to a kick-off form
`WFT.PageLayoutID` → `PageLayout.CodeIssueTypeID` → `CodeIssueType.IsValidFor*`
—
The workflow's attachability is that of its kick-off form's type. `WorkFlowTemplate` itself carries no `IsValidFor*` columns
