ACC-R-050

Lease Accounting & Payments · Approval is irreversible ↑ upgraded

Observed

One-way, and it is the terminus of the workflow.

- Trigger: the ASC 842 Schedule Review/Approval workflow reaches its third step and the client approver accepts (see `ACC-R-060`). - Condition: `SLSummary.IsApproved := true`, and cannot be reversed through the application. - Confidence: Observed — *"Once a schedule has been approved, it cannot be un-approved."* ⚠ Live capture upgrades this rule materially: `IsApproved` is not a checkbox a user ticks at will. It is the terminus of a three-step, three-approver workflow. A rebuild that models approval as a Boolean setter has modelled the wrong thing.
