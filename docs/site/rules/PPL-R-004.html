PPL-R-004

People & Parties · `Employer` is one table serving three business roles

Observed

- Trigger: Any process that treats Vendor, Landlord, or Tenant-as-counterparty as separate entities. - Input: `PaymentTransaction.VendorID` (type `Employer ID`), `LinkProjectEntityContact. Landlord_EmployerID`, `Employer.Is*Vendor` flags. - Condition: No `Vendor`, `Landlord`, or `Tenant`-as-counterparty object exists in the 223-object schema. - Effect: All three business roles resolve to one `Employer` row. A rebuild should not create separate entities that then require manual synchronisation. - Confidence: Observed — 009, this module's field export.
