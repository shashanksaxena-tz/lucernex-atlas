ACC-R-044

Lease Accounting & Payments · Translation vs. revaluation mapping

Observed

A contract flagged 'Is Translation' uses the translation mapping; otherwise the revaluation mapping.

- Condition: `Contract.IsTranslation = true` ⇒ use the Translation mapping on `Admin > Manage Company > Financial Settings`; false ⇒ the Revaluation mapping. - Confidence: Observed, stated verbatim. --- ## G. Modification, remeasurement, impairment
