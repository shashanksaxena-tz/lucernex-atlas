ACC-R-042

Lease Accounting & Payments · Which short-term liability is reported

Observed

A firm-level setting selects the amortisation-based or PV-based figure.

- Trigger: reporting the short-term liability. - Inputs: firm setting Short-Term/Long-Term Liability Calculation Method (`Admin > Manage Company > Financial Settings`). - Condition: `Liability Amortization Based` ⇒ use `Forward12MonthLiabilityAmortBased`; `PV Based` ⇒ use `Forward12MonthLiabilityPVBased`. - Confidence: Observed for the switch; the PV-based formula itself is not available offline.
