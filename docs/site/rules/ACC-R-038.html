ACC-R-038

Lease Accounting & Payments · Interest expense

Observed

Interest on the liability at the discount rate; the balance it is charged on is unconfirmed.

- Computation: `PeriodInterestAmount[n]` = interest owed on the liability, at the discount rate. - Confidence: Observed for the description (*"The interest owed on the liability based on the discount rate"*); Inferred for whether the base is the opening or closing liability balance.
