WF-R-026

Workflow & Approvals · C. Routing resolution

Derived

The step's ApproverJobTitleIDList is intersected against the roster of the entity the workflow is running on, filtered to members holding a matching job title, and the resulting flat member list is frozen onto the running step as WorkFlowStep.ApproverMemberIDList.

Step instantiation
`WFTS.ApproverType` = Job Title; `ApproverJobTitleIDList`; `LinkMemberProjectEntity` for `WF.ProjectEntityID`; `Member.CodeJobTitleID`/`CodeJobTitleIDList`
—
`WFS.ApproverMemberIDList` = every member on the entity holding one of the listed titles
Derived; corroborated by Observed *"The Job Title is used when auto-assigning things like tasks, work flow steps, and notifications"*
