PPL-R-005

People & Parties · `Party` is a generic, classified role assignment, not an identity record

Derived

- Trigger: A role needs recording against a Contract that doesn't fit the standing roster (`LinkProjectEntityContact`) or a direct `Employer` relationship elsewhere on `Contract`. - Input: `Party.CompanyID` (nullable `Employer ID`), `Party.ContactID` (nullable `Contact`, soft), `CodePartyGroupID`/`CodePartyTypeID`, `PrimaryFlag`. - Condition: Both `CompanyID` and `ContactID` are independently nullable. - Effect: A `Party` row can name a company, a person, or (in principle) both, classified by type — the escape hatch for a role that isn't one of the platform's named relationships. - Confidence: Derived — field shape only, no live capture of populated rows.
