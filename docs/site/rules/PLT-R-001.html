PLT-R-001

Platform & Tenancy · Tenant isolation is one join deep

Derived

- Trigger: Any tenant-scoped read or write. - Input: `ProjectEntityID` on the record; `FirmID` on the `ProjectEntity` row it points at. - Condition: The record is one of the 161 `entity_scoped` objects (not `ProjectEntity` itself or one of its 9 subtype roots). - Effect: The record carries no `FirmID` of its own. Tenant scoping must be enforced by joining to `ProjectEntity` and filtering on its `FirmID`, or not at all. - Confidence: Derived — exhaustive field-list check, `../../data-model/project-entity.md` §4.
