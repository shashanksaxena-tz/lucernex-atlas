A rollout program needs capacity tracking
`DevelopmentPlan` → `DevelopmentSlot` → `ProgramRevenueWeeks`
Rolls up filled/unfilled slot and week counts per `Program`. No FK connects a `DevelopmentSlot` to the specific `PotentialProject`/`RETransaction` it is meant to fill — only the generic `ProjectEntityID`.
