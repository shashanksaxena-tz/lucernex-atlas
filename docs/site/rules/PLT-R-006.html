PLT-R-006

Platform & Tenancy · `Security` is a read-only, computed shadow of `UserClassSecurity`

Derived

- Trigger: Any permission check. - Input: `UserClassSecurity` (has a physical table) and `Security` (does not). - Condition: Both declare the same 21 fields. - Effect: `UserClassSecurity` is the editable grant; `Security` is what a rebuild's authorization service would compute and cache, never what an admin edits directly. - Confidence: Derived — exhaustive field-list diff, `data-model.md`.
