ACC-R-035

Lease Accounting & Payments · Total commitment

Observed

Sum of all rent payments over the lease life.

- Computation: `TotalCommitment = Σ` all rent payments for the life of the lease. - Output: `SLSummary.TotalCommitment`. - Confidence: Observed — *"The sum of all rent payments for the life of the lease."*
