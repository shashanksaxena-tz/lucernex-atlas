LAY-R-133

Configuration, Layouts, Forms & Reporting · D. Conditional field display — where the rules are stored

Derived
Conditional rules are invisible to the data API. No type matching `condition`, `rule`, `criteria`, `visib`, `depend`, `trigger`, `expression` or `predicate` exists in the 490-type GraphQL schema, and `PageLayout*` tables are absent from the 223-object business model entirely. Rules are platform configuration, not tenant data.
