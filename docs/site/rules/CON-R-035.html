CON-R-035

Contracts & Leases · 3. Recurring expense: setup and schedule

Derived

The clause is flagged IsDailyRent: the period amount is the daily rate multiplied by the day count in the period, rather than a fixed period amount.

`ExpenseSetup.IsDailyRent = true`
`ExpenseSchedule.DailyRentRate`, period day count
Amount = daily rate × days in period, rather than a fixed period amount
