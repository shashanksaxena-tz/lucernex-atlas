Atlas › Vault › "Caveat: every UI label may be tenant-local"

caveatmethodconfigurationmetaObserved

Manage Firm Dictionary (screen-manage-firm-dictionary) lets a firm overwrite field labels tenant-wide by uploading a spreadsheet — the screen's own note offers it "to provide new translation or to just overwrite the field labels" — with global and firm-specific layers and per-language variants.

This is a caveat on the whole corpus, not one document. Every screen name, navigation node name, field label and code-table value name recorded anywhere may be a tenant-local override rather than the product's own vocabulary.

Internal names are unaffected — ScriptName, CodeContractStatusID, physical table names — which turns the writing convention "use real field and table names in code, never paraphrases" from a style preference into a correctness requirement.

It also explains a measurement error. The census reconciliation reported 18 missing tables when matched on UI label and 6 when matched on physical name; ten of the twelve phantoms were Virtual* views the UI renames, and one was ProjectEntity itself, labelled "General Entity Info". Label-versus-physical-name mismatch is exactly the failure this mechanism produces at scale — see method-cheap-signals.

Whether either tenant has actually overridden anything is unknown. One Download Current Dictionary with Firm specific phrases selected would settle it.
