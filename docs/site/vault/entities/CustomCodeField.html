custom_code_field · 13 fields · layouts-forms-reporting

The meta-definition of a tenant-created custom code or drop-down field — the machinery behind Client Drop Downs.

It carries ParentCustomCodeFieldID (a self-reference) and ParentCustomCodeTableID, so dependent and cascading drop-downs are a real capability.

And all 38 of BBW's firm drop-downs have ParentCustomCodeTableID empty. The capability exists and is used nowhere — a useful negative for anyone sizing the rebuild.

sTYPE_CUSTOM_CODE_FIELD is the second-commonest firm field type, on 54 of them.
