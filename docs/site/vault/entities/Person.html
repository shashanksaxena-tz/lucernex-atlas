A second supertype, alongside ProjectEntity — and the most useful finding in its module.

Person and NonMember are field-for-field identical: 37 fields, 37 matching types, zero differences. Member is that same 37-field block plus 44 login and security columns. All three share PersonID (rule-PPL-R-001).

So the identity aggregate is one supertype with two subtypes on a shared key: someone with a login (Member) and someone without (NonMember), plus the plain contact.

No hard FK type references a person. The 12 columns typed Contact are a soft type, polymorphic into this aggregate (rule-PPL-R-002) — so a schema-driven FK tool sees no relationship at all.
