PRJ-R-005 · module-projects-capital · Derived

Portfolio's default HolidaySchedule and its weekend policy feed task duration arithmetic, overridable per task.

This is the correction that moves the holiday calendar out of accounting. Manage Holiday Calendar sits among the five financial reference-data tools on the admin dashboard and belongs with scheduling — its own help text says holiday days determine task completion dates.

The calendar is empty in BBW, as four of those five tables are — feature-reference-data.

Holiday Calendar is also one of the ten soft reference types, appearing on just 3 columns.
