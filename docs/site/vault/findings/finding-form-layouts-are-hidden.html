Atlas › Vault › Form layouts are a hidden sub-system — and that is where the rules live

findinglayoutsworkflowObserved

The layout registry shows 93 layouts. The real total is 135.

42 form layouts are reachable only through Issue Types. mode=ISSUE and mode=FORM silently fall back to mode=SEP, so they never appear in the registry at all. What makes a layout a form layout is a non-null PageLayoutField.CodeIssueTypeID.

This is not a curiosity — it is where the conditional-field feature actually lives. A sweep of the 93 concluded the feature was "wired everywhere and used nowhere". A sweep of all 135 finds 8 layouts carrying 50 conditional records and 54 criteria clauses, and six of the eight are form layouts:

The feature exists in this tenant to make one request form adapt to its request type. 44 of the 54 clauses are driven by a single field, Issue.LAR_RequestType — which is exactly BRD-24's territory.

The scoping gap was not the only problem: two of the eight populated layouts sit inside the 93 that were swept. The other half of the failure was a probe that could never have observed a rule.
