The boundary is porous in one direction and not the other: only 13 FK columns cross from in-scope objects into Budget/Bid/Cost — nine of them the same BudgetTemplateID column — while 71 point the other way. So cutting the area loses little and keeping it costs little.

Two retired rule ids belong to this area and must not be reused: RPT-R-034 and RPT-R-035.
