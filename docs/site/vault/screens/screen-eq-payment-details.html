The largest subtraction in the module. Payment Info drops five of Contract's twelve screens: Alternate Rent, Invoices, Recoveries, Percentage Rent, Sales.

Every one is retail: recoveries are landlord expense pass-through, percentage rent and sales are turnover rent.

And the rename is the tell. Recurring Expenses → Recurring Payments is not a dropped screen but a reframed one — the same ExpenseSetup machinery, described as an outflow rather than a recovery.
